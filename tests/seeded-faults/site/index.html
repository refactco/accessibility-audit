Fresh food from the harbor

Harbor Market sells fruit, bread, and fish from the boats and the farms around the bay. Read the articles about seasonal food, or go to the list of products.

Free delivery on orders above 40 euros.

Open from 7:00, six days a week.

How it works

  1. Choose your products on the products page.
