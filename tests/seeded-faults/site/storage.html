How to store bread for a week

Sourdough bread stays good for a week when you keep it in a paper bag at room temperature. Do not put it in the refrigerator, because the cold makes it dry.

Day 1 to 3

Keep the loaf with the cut side down on a wooden board.

Day 4 to 7

Cut slices and toast them. Freeze the slices that you do not eat in the first week.
